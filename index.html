Why the town keeps coming back

Forty years in one place
The shop has not moved and has not changed hands. The person who sold medicine to a family's grandfather is still at the counter.
Genuine stock, stored properly
Medicines come from licensed distributors, cold-chain items stay in the fridge, and expiry is checked before anything leaves the counter.
Someone reads the prescription
Doctors' handwriting, dosage, what to take with food, what not to take together — ask, and you will get a straight answer in Hindi.
Urgent medicine gets arranged
If something is not on the shelf, it is ordered from Etah or Agra instead of being refused. Call and ask how soon it can reach.
